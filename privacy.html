Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a travel enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, destination, travel date, number of travellers, trip style, and message.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, plan travel arrangements, provide customer support, and communicate about requested services.

3. Data Storage

Booking information submitted through our website may be stored in our application database. We aim to use appropriate security measures to protect this information.

4. Information Sharing

Information may need to be shared with travel service providers to arrange requested services. We do not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website functionality or analytics. Additional information should be provided if these technologies are enabled.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page.
