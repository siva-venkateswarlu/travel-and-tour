Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please review our refund and cancellation terms before making a travel booking with WanderVista.

Last Updated: [Add Date]

1. Booking Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of their travel booking by contacting the WanderVista support team. Cancellation requests are subject to the applicable booking terms and supplier conditions.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the selected travel package, booking status, cancellation timing, and the policies of airlines, hotels, and other suppliers.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed using the applicable payment method. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and bank.

4. Non-Refundable Services

Certain promotional packages, airline tickets, hotel reservations, and special bookings may be non-refundable. Any such restrictions should be communicated before booking confirmation.

5. Changes to Travel Plans

Requests to change travel dates, destinations, or package details depend on availability and supplier terms. Additional charges may apply.

6. Cancellations by WanderVista

If WanderVista cancels a confirmed booking, customers will be informed about available alternatives and applicable refund options.

7. Contact Us

For cancellation or refund enquiries, please contact our support team.
